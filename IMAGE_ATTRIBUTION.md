### 7. Authentic Aerospace Sound Design (`assets/audio/`)
* **Continuous Spacecraft Interior Ambience (`spacecraft_ambience.wav`):** Continuous deep 48/96/144Hz engine hum, gentle filtered air ventilation turbulence, 400Hz aerospace avionics drone, and distant electronic signal pulses.
* **Systems Startup (`startup.wav`):** Avionics capacitor charge sweep and dual-harmonic harmonic chime upon mission initialization.
* **Engineering Mechanical Switch (`click.wav`):** Rotary switch latch and aerospace relay engagement.
* **Launch Countdown & Ignition (`launch.wav`):** 3-2-1 terminal countdown beeps transitioning into low-frequency chemical rocket combustion rumble.
* **Deep Space Flight Beacon (`flight.wav`):** Navigation signal beacon with subtle ion thruster hiss.
* **Master Caution Klaxon (`alarm.wav`):** Dual-tone alternating aerospace hazard alert.
* **Sensor Reconnaissance Ping (`scan.wav`):** Clean crystalline frequency chirp (1.8 kHz to 3.2 kHz) for planetary surface geological scans.
* **Deep Space Network Downlink (`downlink.wav`):** High-gain carrier lock and FSK digital telemetry packet handshake.
* **Mission Success (`success.wav`):** Uplifting major triad harmonic resolution.
* **Mission Failure (`failure.wav`):** Low-pitched resonant descending alert tone.
