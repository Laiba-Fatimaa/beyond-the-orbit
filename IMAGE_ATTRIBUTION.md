### 7. Authentic Aerospace Sound Design (`assets/audio/`)
* **Audio Engineering:** Custom synthesized procedural aerospace audio waveforms generated for this simulation:
  * `spacecraft_ambience.wav`: Continuous deep 48/96/144Hz engine hum, filtered air ventilation turbulence, 400Hz aerospace avionics drone.
  * `startup.wav`: Avionics capacitor charge sweep and dual-harmonic chime upon mission initialization.
  * `click.wav`: Rotary switch latch and aerospace relay engagement.
  * `launch.wav`: 3-2-1 terminal countdown beeps transitioning into low-frequency chemical rocket combustion rumble.
  * `flight.wav`: Navigation signal beacon with subtle ion thruster hiss.
  * `alarm.wav`: Dual-tone alternating aerospace hazard alert.
  * `scan.wav`: Clean crystalline frequency chirp (1.8 kHz to 3.2 kHz) for planetary surface geological scans.
  * `downlink.wav`: High-gain carrier lock and FSK digital telemetry packet handshake.
  * `success.wav`: Uplifting major triad harmonic resolution.
  * `failure.wav`: Low-pitched resonant descending alert tone.
