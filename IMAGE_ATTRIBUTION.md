# BEYOND THE ORBIT — IMAGE ASSET ATTRIBUTION & SOURCE RECORD
**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 | Team Orbitera**

This project uses authentic NASA, ESA, JAXA, and mission archive photographic imagery. All assets are integrated for non-commercial educational space science and mission design simulation.

---

### 1. Cinematic Landing Screen
* **Primary Earth Horizon Artwork:** Extracted from NASA astronaut photography / ISS Expedition 65 high-resolution orbital horizon imagery (`File:ISS-65_The_atmospheric_glow_blanketing_Earth's_horizon.jpg`).

  * *License:* Public Domain (NASA Work).

### 4. Flight Operations & Reconnaissance Environments
* **Rocky Asteroid Sprites:**
  * *Bennu:* NASA OSIRIS-REx PolyCam global mosaic (`File:Bennu_mosaic_OSIRIS-REx.jpg`). NASA / Goddard / University of Arizona (Public Domain).
  * *Vesta:* NASA Dawn Framing Camera global mosaic (`File:Vesta_-_Dawn_(43193614815).png`). NASA / JPL-Caltech / UCLA / MPS / DLR / IDA (Public Domain).
  * *Ryugu:* JAXA Hayabusa2 ONC-T enhanced color observation (`File:Ryugu_colored.jpg`). JAXA / University of Tokyo / Kochi University (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  * *Eros:* NASA NEAR Shoemaker multispectral imager (`File:Eros_-_PIA02923_(color).jpg`). NASA / JPL / JHUAPL (Public Domain).
* **Surface Reconnaissance Viewfinder Targets:**
  * *Mars Jezero Delta:* NASA Mars 2020 Perseverance / MRO HiRISE camera (`File:260184-JezeroCrater-Delta-Full.jpg`). NASA / JPL-Caltech / University of Arizona (Public Domain).
  * *Moon Shackleton Crater:* NASA LROC & ShadowCam high-resolution mosaic (`File:Shackleton_Crater.jpg`). NASA / KARI / Arizona State University (Public Domain).
  * *Europa Conamara Chaos:* NASA Galileo SSI camera ice-raft mosaic (`File:PIA01296_Conomara_Chaos_regional_view.jpg`). NASA / JPL (Public Domain).
